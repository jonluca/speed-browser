Speed Browser Privacy Policy

Effective June 14, 2026

Speed Browser does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your browsing history, page contents, search queries, or personal information. The app does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

Information stored on your device

Your speed preferences, disabled websites, blocked timer sources, and a short list of recently visited URLs are stored locally on your device. This information is used only to provide the app's browser and acceleration features. You can clear browsing data, restore default settings, or remove all local information by deleting the app.

Websites you visit

Web pages are loaded directly from the websites you choose in an Apple WebKit view. Those websites may collect information under their own privacy policies, just as they would in another browser. Speed Browser does not receive a separate copy of that information.

Network communication

Speed Browser connects only to websites you request and to resources those websites load. The app does not operate an account service or a browsing-data server.

Children

Speed Browser does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, open an issue in the Speed Browser GitHub repository.
